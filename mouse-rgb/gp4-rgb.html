GP4 mouse lighting

Not connected

Use this only after gp4-diagnostic.html showed that the volume test works. Settings last until you unplug the mouse or restart the PC.

Lighting mode

The colour cycle (red, purple, blue, magenta) is fixed in the mouse. You choose the speed, a still light, or off.

Freeze on a colour (experimental)

Restarts the colour cycle, waits, then switches to Static. If "static" holds whatever colour is showing, adjust the wait to land on the colour you like. If the mouse always shows the same colour in static, this trick won't work on your mouse.

Flash effects

These turn the scroll wheel into a "flash" trigger for 6 seconds. Roll the wheel during that time to see the effect. Normal scrolling comes back automatically afterwards.
