GP4 mouse diagnostic

Checks whether your Gammec GP4 (USB 18f8:0f99) understands the Instant A704F commands. Nothing here changes DPI or buttons, and nothing is saved in the mouse. Unplugging the mouse undoes everything.

1Connect

Click Connect mouse. Chrome opens a small list. Pick the entry named USB OPTICAL MOUSE (if there are several, pick any one and click Connect; you can add the others by clicking the button again). Every interface you allow is listed below.

2Read what the mouse holds right now

Best done right after you unplug and replug the mouse, before sending anything. If it returns something other than zeros, that's a clue about the firmware. This only reads.

3Press the mouse's buttons

The page is now listening to this interface. Press the DPI button (usually behind the wheel), then each side button, one at a time. Watch the log.

On the A704F, this interface sends report 06 for the DPI button and report 02 for media keys. If you see IN report 06 … lines, your mouse has the same layout as the A704F. That is a good sign, but it doesn't prove the same commands work.

4The key test: volume scrolling

This sends 07 11 01 00 00 00 00 00, which on an A704F turns the scroll wheel into a volume knob.

  1. Click Turn on volume scrolling.
  2. Move the pointer over this page and roll the wheel a few notches.
  3. Watch for the Windows volume pop-up, or check whether the page still scrolls.
  4. Answer below. Scrolling goes back to normal by itself after 30 seconds, or right away when you answer.

Did rolling the wheel change the volume?

The page saw volume key reports coming from the mouse, so the command worked.

5Lighting tests (only useful if step 4 worked)

5a. Turn the LED off, then back on. Sends 13 7f 17 (off). After 3 seconds it sends 13 7f 10 (slow breathing).

5b. Flash test. On the A704F the "debug" scroll values seem to fire when you roll the wheel, not when they are sent. This sends 11 02 09 (wheel = flash LED). Roll the wheel during the 8-second countdown. Normal scrolling comes back automatically afterwards.
